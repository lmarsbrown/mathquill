Iterate operator — \iterate{g=0}_{k=1}^{n}

Loads build/mathquill.js directly. Rebuild with node build.js and hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) after any change.

How to type it

  1. Click into the field below and type \iterate then a space.
  2. The cursor lands in the seed slot — type g=0.
  3. Down for the lower bound, Up for the upper bound.
  4. Right out of the operator, then type the body (e.g. g+k^2) — it is trailing content, not a slot.
